# About David Sudher

## A Life Rooted in Prayer
David Sudher was born and raised in Bangalore, India, in a home deeply rooted in prayer and the presence of God.

From his earliest years, David was surrounded by a strong spiritual atmosphere. His mother was a woman of prayer who would spend **seven hours a day in prayer**, seeking God and interceding for others. Her life of devotion became one of the earliest influences that shaped David's relationship with God.

Her prayers, sacrifices, and commitment to God played a significant role in raising a son who would go on to be used powerfully by God and become a blessing to many.

The foundation laid in his home created in David a deep awareness of God's presence and a desire to know Him personally. What began as the spiritual atmosphere of his childhood would eventually become a personal pursuit of God's presence.

## A Growing Hunger for God
As David grew in his relationship with God, his hunger and thirst for Him continued to increase.

In 2010, David developed an unquenchable desire to experience the fullness of the Holy Spirit.

During this season, David's hunger for God became so intense that he went to his mentor, **Joshua Paul**, seeking guidance and asking how he could go deeper in his relationship with God and experience the fullness of the Holy Spirit.

As Joshua Paul spoke with David and began giving him instructions to devote himself to the Scriptures and spend time in fasting and prayer, something extraordinary happened.

**While they were still in conversation, before David could even carry out the instructions he had been given, God saw the deep hunger and unquenchable thirst in David's heart and filled him with the Holy Spirit.**

In that very moment, David experienced a powerful encounter with God. His spiritual eyes were opened, and he began to experience the spiritual realm in a way he had never experienced before.

David describes seeing many generals of God, including **Billy Graham, Kathryn Kuhlman, and many others**.

This encounter became a defining moment in David's spiritual journey. It showed him that God sees the hunger of a person's heart and responds to those who genuinely seek Him.

From this experience came a statement that continues to express David's conviction:

> **"When there is an unquenchable thirst for God, He will fill you."**

## A Journey into Prophetic Ministry
Following this powerful encounter, David became part of the **Circle Prayer Fellowship**, where his pursuit of God continued to grow.

Although he was deeply involved in prayer and fellowship, there remained a persistent desire within him to understand the prophetic and to learn how God speaks, leads, and reveals His purposes to His people.

His hunger continued to draw him toward prophetic teaching and deeper spiritual formation.

Between **2012 and 2013**, David experienced significant prophetic confirmations concerning his calling and future ministry.

During this season, words were spoken over his life concerning an **apostolic calling and a prophetic assignment to the nations**.

These confirmations became important markers in David's journey, strengthening his conviction that God had called him not merely to experience His presence, but to become a vessel through which others could encounter Him.

## Raising the Next Generation
One of the defining expressions of David's ministry has been his investment in the younger generation.

For approximately **10 years**, David served and led teams of young people, investing his time, energy, teaching, prayer, and leadership into their lives.

His desire was never simply to build a team. His heart was to raise people who would know God, hear His voice, recognize their calling, and walk confidently in the purposes of God for their lives.

Through years of leadership, hundreds of young people have been impacted and blessed through the teams and communities David has served.

David believes that when people are intentionally discipled and positioned under godly leadership, they can discover what God has placed within them and be equipped to influence their generation.

> **"When God raises people, He doesn't raise them only for themselves. He raises them to become a blessing to others."**

His investment in the next generation continues to be a significant part of his ministry.

A central burden in David's ministry is to help people develop a deeper relationship with God.

## From Prophet to Leader
God's calling upon David's life as a prophet has also developed him as a leader to many and has positioned him to raise other leaders for God.

A prophet proclaims what God reveals, while a leader helps guide people toward the purposes and direction God has placed before them.

David has spent years leading people toward God, encouraging them to pursue Him wholeheartedly and helping them discover and walk in the purpose God has for their lives.

His leadership is centered on helping people understand their calling, recognize the direction God is leading them, and take steps of faith toward fulfilling that purpose.

Through his leadership, David has seen many people grow spiritually, discover their gifts, step into leadership, and become leaders who can, in turn, influence others for God.

## A Ministry That Crosses Borders
What began in Bangalore has continued to extend beyond geographical boundaries.

David has ministered in **cities and villages across India and in Pakistan**, carrying a message of faith, prayer, the Word of God, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Over the years, David's ministry and leadership have touched lives in different parts of the world, including the **United States**, where people have been encouraged and blessed through his ministry.

David has received testimonies from people who have experienced God's intervention in their lives, including testimonies of physical healing, breakthrough, and transformation.

These testimonies are not viewed as a reason to glorify a person, but as reminders of God's grace and His ability to touch, restore, and transform lives.
