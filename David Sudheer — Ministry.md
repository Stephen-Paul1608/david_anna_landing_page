# Ministry

### Called to Impact a Generation

David's ministry is rooted in a passion to see young people discover Christ, grow in their relationship with Him, and become powerful witnesses for the Gospel.

His journey in ministry began through mentoring a youth group, which grew into a broader calling to serve and guide young people in India and around the world. Through teaching, mentoring, prayer, and personal discipleship, his desire is to help people encounter Christ and understand God's purpose for their lives.

### A Voice for the Next Generation

David carries a strong conviction to raise, encourage, and equip the next generation to live boldly for Christ. He believes that young people are not simply the church of tomorrow, but a generation God can use powerfully today.

His ministry focuses on:

- Mentoring and discipling young people
- Guiding people toward a deeper relationship with Christ
- Encouraging believers to become bold witnesses for the Gospel
- Equipping the next generation to discover and walk in God's calling
- Ministering through teaching, prayer, encouragement, and personal guidance

### Ministry Beyond Borders

David has had the opportunity to minister in multiple places across **India, Pakistan, and the United States**, connecting with people from different backgrounds and cultures.

Across every place and community, his heart remains the same: **to point people to Jesus Christ, strengthen their faith, and encourage them to become a powerful witness for Him.**

> **“Be a powerful witness. Lead people to Christ. Raise a generation that will live boldly for Him.”**
