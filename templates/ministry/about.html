About

David Sudher

Leader. Mentor. Minister. Witness.

David Sudher brings more than 17 years of experience in leadership, ministry, and people development, with a heart to help believers know Christ and live with purpose.

He has served in sales, operations, and global leadership spaces, but the deepest influence in his life has been the call to raise a generation that knows God, hears His voice, and moves with courage.

A Life Rooted in Prayer

David Sudher was born and raised in Bangalore, India, in a home deeply rooted in prayer and the presence of God.

From his earliest years, he was shaped by the life of his mother, a woman of prayer who spent seven hours a day in prayer. Her devotion became one of the earliest influences that shaped his relationship with God.

Her prayers and sacrifice laid a foundation for David to grow in spiritual sensitivity and a deep hunger for God's presence.

A Growing Hunger for God

As David grew in his relationship with God, his hunger and thirst for Him continued to increase.

In 2010, he developed an unquenchable desire to experience the fullness of the Holy Spirit. After seeking wise counsel and devoting himself to prayer and Scripture, he experienced a powerful encounter with God.

God answered the hunger of his heart in a way that marked the rest of his life.

"When there is an unquenchable thirst for God, He will fill you."

Raising the Next Generation

One of the defining expressions of David's ministry has been his investment in the younger generation.

For approximately 10 years, David served and led teams of young people, investing his time, teaching, prayer, and leadership into their lives.

His heart was never merely to build a team. It was to raise people who would know God, hear His voice, and walk confidently in His purposes.

"When God raises people, He raises them to become a blessing to others."

Equipping People to Hear God

A central burden in David's ministry is to help people develop a deeper relationship with God.

He longs to see believers move beyond knowing about God and begin to pray, study Scripture, recognize God's voice, discern His leading, and understand their spiritual calling.

His ministry emphasizes hunger for God, prayer, the Word, the Holy Spirit, and spiritual leadership.

A Ministry That Crosses Borders

What began in Bangalore has continued beyond geographical boundaries.

David has ministered in cities and villages across India and Pakistan, and he has also ministered in the United States, carrying a message of faith, prayer, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Across every place, the heart of the message remains the same: to help people encounter Jesus and step into their calling.

The Journey Continues

David's story is still being written.

From the prayers of a home in Bangalore to a growing ministry reaching lives across nations, his journey is a testimony of what God can do when a person yields to Him with an undivided heart.

“To remain hungry for God, faithful to His calling, and available for His purposes.”
