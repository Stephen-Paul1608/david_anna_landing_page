# David Sudheer

### Vice President, Global Sales & Customer Success

David Sudheer brings more than 17 years of experience across Sales, Operations, Strategic Planning, and Leadership. In his role as Vice President, Global Sales & Customer Success, he is responsible for developing new business and establishing DBSync as a trusted partner for its customers.

With extensive experience in technical B2B solution sales, SaaS, and Cloud Computing, David combines business expertise with a strong passion for people, leadership, and customer success.

Beyond his professional career, David is deeply committed to ministry and to seeing the next generation encounter Jesus Christ and walk boldly in their calling.
